# Data, templates and model attribution

This academic experiment includes a selected BEIR-formatted Natural Questions passage subset. Natural Questions was created by Kwiatkowski et al. (2019); the BEIR conversion is credited to Thakur et al. (2021). Wikipedia passage authors are the contributors to the articles identified in `evidence/reference_audit/nq_passage_attribution.csv`. The official BEIR/NQ dataset card declares CC BY-SA 4.0; Google's original Natural Questions release declares CC BY-SA 3.0. Both source notices and official license URLs are retained in `evidence/reference_audit/source_records/`. These passages are not licensed by the unrelated MIT or Apache licenses of utility code.

The project selected passages and added retrieval scores, split labels and evaluation metadata. Source text in the compact input subset was not intentionally rewritten; the benign-echo control explicitly adds a question prefix, while the benign-mirror control copies source passages with new record IDs. These transformations are marked by control family and documented in the protocol. They do not imply endorsement by the source authors. Article/history URLs in the attribution CSV were reconstructed from retained titles; original Wikipedia revision IDs were not supplied and have not been invented.

PoisonedRAG attack material credits Zou, Geng, Wang and Jia and retains its MIT notice, copyright Runpeng Geng, in `improved_generation/inputs/PoisonedRAG_LICENSE.txt`. Adapted attack templates also credit Open-Prompt-Injection and CleanBase with their MIT and Apache-2.0 notices under `evidence/reference_audit/source_records/`. Experimental factual corruption and injected instructions must not be represented as statements made or endorsed by Wikipedia contributors. The independently retained source cards, immutable repository revisions and byte hashes are listed in the evidence files.

Model weights are not bundled. Contriever's retained source notice declares CC BY-NC 4.0; the DeBERTa guard retains its Apache-2.0 notice. These model notices and the NotInject/PIGuard MIT notice are under `improved_experiment/licenses/`. Qwen's fixed model card/source metadata and file hashes are under `improved_generation/` and `evidence/reference_audit/source_records/`. Downloading weights does not change the source data or model terms.

The later user-supplied Drive archive restores the historical NQ generated responses and their embedded prompts. They are retained under `evidence/provided_3b_rescoring/raw/`; Wikipedia passages and adapted attack material appearing inside those prompts retain the attribution above. Forty-eight logical policy/condition sources map to thirty-three distinct physical files, with every original file hash recorded. These are supplied historical records, not a newly verified Qwen checkpoint or T4 execution. Downloaded upstream repositories, their Git histories and duplicate bounded-output files are omitted from the merged delivery.


## Revised source-disjoint transfer suite

The selected SQuAD v1.1 material is from the official Stanford release. Original article titles, paragraph IDs, questions, answer annotations, source URLs and dataset SHA-256 are retained in `revised_experiment/data/`. The frozen custom split contains30 development,100 calibration and100 test articles with179/600/600 paragraphs. It is not an official SQuAD leaderboard split or an independent verification of current world facts. Dataset license and attribution follow CC BY-SA4.0 as documented in the source README; paragraph-level Wikipedia sources are included. The original full release is downloadable from its recorded URL, not duplicated here.

Our controlled false-evidence and instruction strings are locally constructed from the recorded source passages with independently reviewed same-type alternative answers. They are not author-produced CorruptRAG attacks. Targets and source-quality masks were frozen before final-test responses. All100test cases remain available;85source-supported cases are separately identified.

The official Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct-GGUF Q4_K_M weights are pinned by immutable revision and SHA-256. Its Qwen Research license is included as `revised_experiment/GENERATOR_LICENSE.txt`; weights are not redistributed. The frozen Contriever/DeBERTa source manifests and original licenses remain included. Runtime dependencies and model weights are installed/downloaded separately.

The later matched-control extension reuses exactly these declared source passages, frozen questions and transformations. It adds the previously missing unfiltered generation requests without introducing a second dataset or changing data licenses. The literature review distributes derived reading notes, source links and hashes rather than full-paper PDFs or downloaded HTML/text.
